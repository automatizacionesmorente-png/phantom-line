18. **WSOLA loses the pitch of a 100 Hz voice (found by N15, front-desk-brain task 4).** With the spectrum oracle, N15 finds real pitch changes in `lib/voice/timeStretch.ts`, both with the dominant tone at exactly 100 Hz, whose 10 ms period equals the whole ±5 ms search width. Seed 13 run 124 (100 Hz plus two 400 Hz tones, rate 1.25, 525 ms): the output has no 100 Hz at all; its energy moves to 75 Hz and 125 Hz (0.36 each), a 25 Hz flutter. Seed 9 run 80 (100 Hz with 230 Hz and 276 Hz, rate 1.029, 629 ms): the output peak is 103 Hz, and its zero crossings rise from 98.4 to 101.6 per second. Seen in 2 of 3000 generated cases, so N15 fails about 1 suite run in 10 until this is fixed. Low male voices sit near 100 Hz, so this matters for the live neural voice at rate 1.1. Fixing it changes the approved "±5 ms search" of natural-voice design.md ("Pace"), so it is the owner's call; the property's 2 % limit and generators stay as they are.
