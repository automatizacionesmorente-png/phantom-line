# Voice-over takes (English, owner's voice)

Record each take as its own clip, 2 seconds of silence before and after, calm pace (about 150 words a minute), at a table in a quiet room with the phone 20 cm from your mouth. Say the takes in order. If you stumble, stay silent for a second and say the whole take again; do not stop the recording.

1. "Every missed call at a small shop is a customer who hung up. So we brought the answering machine back."
2. "Lola runs her salon alone. While she cuts hair, nobody picks up and the booking is gone. Phantom Line answers for her, by voice, in Spanish or English, with her real hours and prices."
3. "I asked for nine at night. The model only reads what I said. The code checks hours and chairs, and offers the nearest free time."
4. "Seconds later Lola's machine blinks. One tap plays the call back. She sees who called, what was agreed and a reply she can send, and the booking is already in her day. The database itself refuses double bookings."
5. "Lola's app is more than a tape. In the morning she sees her day, one column per stylist, and adds or blocks time with one tap. Each client has a card: allergies, birthday, colour formula. At month end she sees what came in, what went out, what her receptionist did, and sends it to her accountant. Five switches prepare reminders and reviews; she sends each with one tap on WhatsApp, nothing automatic. Sample data, a demo month."
6. "English callers get the same rules, the same agenda and a second chair."
7. "Every feature started as a Kiro spec. I reviewed each like a shop owner, and Kiro found where my own changes contradicted its requirements."
8. "Kiro built the core until its credits ran out on the seventeenth of November. Claude wrote the rest, including the salon app, under my direction, and every commit says so. The salon ideas come from our agency's earlier private app; no code was copied."
9. "Phantom Line is also a startup: about four cents a call, a planned price, not market-tested, and every number cites its source. I'm Miguel Morente, a student at UFV Madrid."
10. "Phantom Line. Nobody hangs up on a silent shop again."
