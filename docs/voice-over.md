# Voice-over takes (English, owner's voice)

One clip per take, 2 seconds of silence before and after, calm pace (about 150 words a minute), phone 20 cm from your mouth. If you stumble, stay silent a second and redo the whole take. Take 1 is the hook: say it slowly, with a pause after "Nobody picks up."

1. "Right now, a customer is calling a small shop. Nobody picks up. And that booking is gone forever."
2. "Lola cuts hair alone, so the phone rings out. We brought back the eighties answering machine, and gave it a brain. It answers in Spanish or English, with her real hours and prices."
3. "I ask for nine at night. The AI only listens. The code decides: it checks the hours and the chairs, and offers the nearest free time."
4. "Seconds later, her machine blinks. One tap plays the call: who called, what was agreed. The booking is already in her day, and the database itself refuses double bookings."
5. "And it is a whole salon app. In the morning she sees her day, one column per stylist, and adds or blocks time with one tap. Each client has a card: allergies, birthday, colour formula. At month end she sees what came in, what went out, and what her receptionist did, ready for her accountant. Reminders and reviews are one tap on WhatsApp, nothing automatic. This is a demo month: sample data."
6. "Same brain, in English. Same rules, same agenda, a second chair."
7. "Every feature started as a Kiro spec. I reviewed each one like a shop owner."
8. "Kiro built the core until its credits ran out on the seventeenth of November. Claude wrote the rest, including the salon app, under my direction, and every commit says so. The salon ideas come from our agency's earlier private app; no code was copied."
9. "Phantom Line is also a startup: about four cents a call, a planned price, not market-tested, every number sourced. I'm Miguel Morente, a student at UFV Madrid."
10. "Phantom Line. Nobody hangs up on a silent shop again."
